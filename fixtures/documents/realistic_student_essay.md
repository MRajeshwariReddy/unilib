# The Economics of Renewable Energy Transition

The global transition toward renewable energy sources represents one of the most critical economic and environmental shifts of the twenty-first century.

## Market Forces and Subsidies

Historically, fossil fuels benefited from decades of infrastructure investment and economies of scale. However, levelized cost of energy (LCOE) metrics demonstrate that solar photovoltaic and onshore wind have achieved parity or superiority over traditional coal generation.

## Policy Recommendations

1. Implement predictable carbon pricing mechanisms.
2. Expand grid energy storage research grants.
3. Facilitate international cross-border energy trade.
